# Partner Finder — End-to-End Candidate Flow

1. Candidate opens public Partner Finder landing page.
2. Candidate completes 12 questions.
3. Browser calculates/display-previews informational work profile.
4. Candidate sees profile and capacity summary.
5. Candidate chooses whether to request information about collaboration.
6. If yes, contact form asks only for needed contact data.
7. Server validates the request and creates a candidate under the correct tenant.
8. Candidate starts with status: new.
9. Agent dashboard refreshes and shows the candidate.
10. Agent opens candidate detail.
11. Agent can change status, add notes and schedule follow-up.
12. AI preparation uses only the candidate's submitted answers/profile plus agent-provided notes.
13. AI output is framed as conversation preparation, not an employment suitability decision.
14. Follow-up and onboarding remain controlled by the agent/manager.

Future integrations:
- email provider
- calendar
- CRM
- WhatsApp/business messaging where legally and technically appropriate
